Running a large language model on your own laptop used to require a data center. Today, a quantized seven-billion-parameter model fits comfortably in eight gigabytes of video memory.

The main advantage is privacy. Your documents never leave the machine, and there is no monthly bill. The trade-off is speed: local inference is slower than a hosted API.

What you can do with it

  • Summarize long reports without uploading them to a cloud service.
  • Translate web pages while you read them, offline.
